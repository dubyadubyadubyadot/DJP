Spring Psalm

The fields are like rivers now. Have you bathed in their waters? Sun, pour out on me and may your light radiate through me Through fingers and necks and nailbeds. Spring rush your waters to my pits! Find my corners and dark places, Sun. Pour into me, you sacred bearer of god. Jupiter dance and Saturn hum, Neptune awaken my dreams, may they teem and tendril in green imaginations. Breeze, push these waters through my nostrils, into my eye sockets, through and around my ears, the calloused pads of my hands that show me your face. Moss furl and flower. Moss, have sex! Shadowy birds, have sex! Rocks, have sex! Sun tangle with grass and tree and make them bow and groan then Thrust through the heaving earth. All things, awaken and find that part of you before climax, Hard winter body giving up its ice and sharp edges dissolving into mist Floating there above the rock bed, above his rock hand finding and awakening the deepest part of you. Arise, clouds and laugh. How a hand goes through you! Make me mist in light. Sun make me a flower. This green plant of my body will pour into spring. I will not be boxed. I will not be shipped. I will not be purchased or named. The earth now is a bird’s nest. Sun, god, jupiter— Crack me open.


To a Green Land

Throws it back

Where I stand
The water, gelatin of life, thickens then slacks
For a moment I God’s green blade
Might stand upon these waters
Am I not a boat?
Am I not tethered to the distant shore of my mind?
This rust blood breeze carries
Shell shards
Seagull dung
Gabriel’s first curse
Birds, fallen angels, search this aortic air for escape routes
And I
Less alive than the ocean
